About CapybaraNet LLC

Our mission, principles, and what we stand for

Our Mission

CapybaraNet LLC exists to help people navigate the modern information environment with critical thinking, transparency, and digital self-defense.

We believe democracy depends on informed citizens, trustworthy information, and the ability to distinguish authentic reporting from manipulation, propaganda, and coordinated disinformation campaigns.

Our goal is not to tell people what to think. Our goal is to help people learn how to verify information, evaluate sources, recognize manipulation tactics, and think critically online.

In an era of algorithmic amplification, viral misinformation, AI-generated media, and surveillance advertising, digital literacy is no longer optional — it is essential.

Digital Rights

Every person owns their digital identity and personal information.

  • Personal data belongs to the individual
  • No company should sell or exploit personal data without informed consent
  • Citizens must be able to delete, download, and control their own data
  • Surveillance advertising should be banned or severely restricted
  • Users should have the right to voluntarily license data in exchange for compensation
  • Algorithms affecting public life should be transparent and auditable

Human attention is not a resource to be endlessly harvested.
Technology should serve humanity — not manipulate it.

Truth and Information Integrity

Democracy cannot survive in an environment of mass manipulation and engineered falsehood.

  • Transparency in media ownership and funding
  • Labeling of AI-generated media and deepfakes
  • Publicly accountable fact-checking systems
  • Open-source recommendation algorithms where possible
  • Protection against coordinated disinformation campaigns
  • Strong public-interest journalism
  • Media literacy education

Freedom of speech is essential. Freedom from deception at industrial scale is also essential.

What We Research

CapybaraNet studies and documents:

  • Disinformation campaigns
  • Propaganda tactics
  • Coordinated inauthentic behavior
  • Bot amplification networks
  • Media bias and source credibility
  • AI-generated misinformation
  • Digital manipulation techniques
  • Online influence operations
  • Fact-checking and verification methods

We encourage skepticism toward all information sources, including our own. Credibility comes from evidence, transparency, and accountability.

Our Principles

  • Evidence matters
  • Sources matter
  • Transparency matters
  • Corrections matter
  • Intellectual honesty matters

We reject manipulation regardless of political affiliation, ideology, government, corporation, or organization.

The internet should empower human knowledge — not exploit human psychology.

Learn, Verify, Think Critically

CapybaraNet is dedicated to promoting digital literacy, investigative thinking, and informed civic participation.

Truth requires transparency. Critical thinking requires curiosity. A healthy society requires both.
